Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Scope

This policy applies to a private, non-commercial software utility used by a single individual on their own device. It has no other users and is not offered to the public.

Data collected

None. The software does not collect, transmit to third parties, publish, sell or share any personal data. It contains no advertising, tracking or analytics.

Data accessed

The software acts only on behalf of its owner, using accounts the owner has explicitly authorised, and only on data already belonging to the owner. Any data it handles is processed and stored solely on the owner's own device and within the owner's own accounts.

Sharing

No data is shared with, transferred to, or accessible by any third party.

Limited use

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data is retained only for as long as the owner wishes. The owner may delete it at any time by removing the affected files and revoking the software's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access immediately terminates the software's access to the account.

Security

Access is granted via OAuth 2.0 tokens issued to the owner, stored with restricted permissions on the owner's device. No other person's data is processed.
